Standard bank transfers take 1 to 3 business days to arrive.
Instant transfers arrive within 30 seconds and cost a 1% fee.
Customers can freeze a lost card from the mobile app at any time.
Accounts with more than three failed login attempts are locked for 24 hours.
Identity verification requires a passport or national ID card.
Transactions above 5000 dollars are flagged for manual compliance review.
Refunds for disputed card payments are processed within 10 business days.
Support is available by chat from 8am to 8pm on weekdays.
Two-factor authentication can be enabled in the security settings.
Monthly account statements are sent by email on the first day of each month.
